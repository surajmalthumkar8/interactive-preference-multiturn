✅ Correct Answer: 
         • S1 → Extend or rework the conversation
         • S2 → Stop — the conversation is complete
      
Explanation:

The correct action is always a function of two things: the project's turn requirement and the conversation's naturalness requirement. Multi-Turn Generalist needs 10–15 turns, so S1 must extend or rework earlier turns — at turn 6 the minimum isn't met, and because the conversation already feels complete, the fix isn't tacking on forward questions but enriching the earlier turns so that reaching turn 10 feels natural.
