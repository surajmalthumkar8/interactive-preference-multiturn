📚 Training & Screening


This page explains what you need to know and complete before becoming eligible for production on Interactive Preference | Multi-Turn.
Use this page as your onboarding checklist and navigation guide. The linked project resources contain the detailed instructions you are expected to follow.

🎯 Your goal

Before you can contribute to production, you need to understand the project's requirements and demonstrate that you can apply them correctly.
The onboarding process therefore has two main components:

1. Training & preparation
2. Screening

✅ Before taking the screening

Make sure you:

* Have reviewed the project guidelines.
* Have attended any required webinars or training sessions.
* Have understood the requirements for producing 10–15-turn conversations.
* Have understood how to evaluate and select a preferred response.
* Understand the overall quality and compliance expectations.


If you are unsure whether you have completed the required preparation, check the current onboarding instructions or ask in the channel.

2. 📝 Screening

The screening is the final step of the onboarding process before production eligibility is determined.
It is designed to evaluate whether you can apply the project's requirements and quality standards in practice.

What to expect

You will be asked to complete the project screening according to the instructions provided by the project team.
The screening should be completed independently and according to the applicable project guidelines.

:linked_paperclips:  Click Here to Take the Project Screening

3. 📬 After submitting your screening

Your submission will be reviewed as soon as possible, and you will be informed of the outcome via Slack.

If you are eligible

You will be granted the permissions and credentials required for production and added to the relevant production tools.
You will then be able to proceed to Production Ops.

If you are not eligible

Your screening outcome will be communicated to you via Slack.
If applicable, you may be able to retake the screening according to the project's retake policy.

4. 🧭 While you're waiting

You do not need to remain in the channel waiting for a response.
Keep an eye on Slack for your screening outcome and any project-wide updates.
If you have a question about the onboarding process, check the Project Hub and this page first.
For questions unrelated to this project, such as payments or global platform permissions, use the general onboarding resources or #all-trainner-hub.
