
🚀 Contributor Journey

This page provides an overview of what to expect throughout your participation in Interactive Preference | Multi-Turn, from allocation through production.

You do not need to memorize every step. Use this page as a reference to understand
where you are
what comes next, and
where to find the information you need.
🗺️ At a glance
Your journey can be divided into two main phases:
Phase 1 — Onboarding
Allocation → Channel Access → Project Orientation → Training → Screening
Phase 2 — Production Ops
Screening Outcome → Production Access → Task Claiming → Production → Submission → Ongoing Quality & Compliance

Your participation in the project ends when the project wraps or when your access is removed due to performance or behavioral issues.
🚀 Phase 1 — Onboarding
1. Allocation
You are allocated to the project and added to the project channel. That's why you're here!
This channel is your main source of project-specific information, communication, and support.
2. Project Orientation
Start by reviewing the Project Hub to understand:
what the project is about;
what your contributor journey looks like;
what is expected from you;
where to find project resources;
where to go when you need help.
3. Training
Before taking the screening, make sure you complete or review the required project training materials.
These may include:
project guidelines;
training modules;
webinars;
examples and other reference materials.
See Training & Screening for the current requirements and available resources.
4. Screening
The screening evaluates whether you are ready to contribute to production according to the project's quality and compliance expectations.
Your screening submission will be reviewed, and you will be informed of the outcome via Slack.
5. Screening outcome
If you are eligible:
You will receive the permissions and credentials required for production and will be added to the relevant production tools.
If you are not eligible:
Your eligibility and any applicable retake options will be communicated to you according to the screening process.
🛠️ Phase 2 — Production Ops
6. Production access
Once you are eligible for production, you will receive the required permissions and credentials and be added to
Vercel
Feather
At this point, you can begin participating in production.
7. Task claiming
Use the production workflow to find and claim available tasks as specified in the Project Guidelines — follow the current guidelines when claiming, working on, editing, or releasing tasks.
See Production Ops for detailed procedures.
8. Task production
Your task is to produce a high-quality, diverse 10–15-turn conversation and make the required preference judgments according to the project guidelines.
Quality, compliance, and adherence to project requirements are expected throughout production.
9. Submission
Review your work against the applicable project requirements before submitting.
Once submitted, your work enters the project's Quality Check process.
There is no contributor-to-contributor review layer:
Individual tasks are not assigned to another contributor for review, and contributors are not expected to review one another's work.
10. Ongoing participation
While contributing, you may receive project-specific guidance, quality feedback, or other communications from the project team.
You are responsible for staying up to date with project instructions and announcements posted in this channel.
🏁 How participation ends
Your participation in production can end in two ways:
Project wrap
The project reaches its planned end and production is wrapped.
Removal from production
Your access may be removed if your performance or behavior does not meet project expectations, including issues such as consistently poor-quality submissions or violations of applicable behavioral rules.
