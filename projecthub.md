🎯 About the project
Interactive Preference Multi-Turn (MT) is a project focused on producing a diverse dataset of 10–15-turn conversations.
Contributors create prompts and evaluate two model responses by selecting the response they prefer.

Your relation with the project has two main phases:
1. 🚀 Onboarding
Learn about the project, complete the required training, and take the screening.
2. 🛠️ Production Ops
Contributors who pass the screening receive the required access and can begin claiming and completing production tasks.
🗺️ Your contributor journey
Your journey through the project follows this general path:
Allocation → Channel → Onboarding → Screening → Production → Project Wrap
🚀 Phase 1 — Onboarding
After allocation and channel access, you will:
Learn how the project works.
Review the project guidelines and required resources.
Attend or complete the relevant training materials.
Take the project screening.
Your screening result will determine whether you move into production.
🛠️ Phase 2 — Production Ops
If you pass the screening:
You will receive the required permissions and credentials.
You will be added to the relevant production tools.
You can begin completing tasks, that is, submiting conversations according to the project guidelines.
Your work will be evaluated by the project team for quality and compliance:
 The project has an organic Quality Check (QC) mechanism to monitor submitted work. There is no separate contributor-to-contributor review layer, meaning that you should not expect an individual reviewer to be assigned to each of your tasks, nor will you be expected to review other contributors’ work. 

Your participation in production continues until the project wraps or your access is removed if performance or behavioral issues are detected.
